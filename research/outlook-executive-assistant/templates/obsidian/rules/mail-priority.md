# Mail priority rules

## P0

- Active security incident
- Payment fraud or executive impersonation
- Material legal deadline today

## P1

- Decision or reply required today
- Another person is blocked on the user's response

## P2

- Action required within three business days

## P3

- No current action

The word `urgent` does not determine priority by itself.
