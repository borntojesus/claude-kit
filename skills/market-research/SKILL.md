# Market Research

When triggered, perform research using WebSearch. Ask what to research if not specified.

## Research types:

### Keyword Research

- Search for "[topic] healthcare marketing keywords 2026"
- Find search volume estimates, difficulty, intent (informational/transactional)
- Group keywords into clusters (pillar + supporting)
- Suggest blog topics for each cluster
- Map keywords to existing service pages

### Competitor Analysis

- Search for competitor websites in the medical marketing space
- Analyze their content strategy, blog frequency, social presence
- Identify content gaps we can fill
- Note their unique value propositions

### Industry Trends

- Search for "healthcare marketing trends 2026"
- Identify emerging channels, technologies, patient behavior shifts
- Suggest how to adapt our content/services

### Content Gap Analysis

- Compare our blog topics vs what's ranking in search
- Find high-value topics we haven't covered
- Prioritize by search volume and business relevance

## Output format:

- Executive summary (3 sentences)
- Key findings (bullet list)
- Actionable recommendations with priority (High/Medium/Low)
- Suggested next steps
